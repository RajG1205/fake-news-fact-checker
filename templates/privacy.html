This page explains the information handled when you use VerifyAI. It is a general description of this application, not a legal guarantee. Please contact the site operator if you need clarification.

Information you submit

When you submit a claim, headline or text for checking, the application sends that content to its server to process the request. The server may use configured search and language-model providers to retrieve and evaluate evidence. Do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.

Technical information

The application may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to operate the service and enforce request limits. The application does not require an account to use the homepage fact-check feature.

Third-party providers

External providers used for search, analysis, fonts or news feeds may receive requests needed to provide their services and may apply their own privacy policies. VerifyAI does not control those providers.

Retention and security

Request processing and service logs may be retained by the hosting or provider systems according to their configuration and policies. The operator should review deployment and provider retention settings before making specific retention promises. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can avoid submitting personal or confidential information and stop using the service at any time. For privacy questions, contact the site operator using the contact details published by the operator, if available.
